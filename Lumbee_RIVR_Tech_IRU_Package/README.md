## 1. Project overview

This package structures a public–private broadband partnership in which:

- **The Lumbee Tribe** — *Lumbee Tribe of North Carolina* (or a designated Tribal entity) —
  applies for and holds a **Tribal Broadband Connectivity Program (TBCP) Round 3**
  award from **NTIA**, and **owns** all grant-funded broadband infrastructure
  (the *Tribal Assets*). Title stays with the Lumbee Tribe.
- **RIVR Tech** — *LREMC Technologies, LLC d/b/a RIVR Tech* — is the Lumbee Tribe's
  **development and operating partner**: it designs, engineers, constructs,
  activates, operates, and maintains the network and sells residential, business,
  enterprise, and voice services over it.
- The grant-funded plant interconnects with RIVR Tech's **existing middle-mile /
  backbone / core**, producing a **hybrid network** with a clear **demarcation**
  between Tribally owned (grant-funded) and RIVR Tech-owned assets.
- RIVR Tech receives a long-term **Indefeasible Right of Use (IRU)** — modeled at
  **20 / 25 / 30 years** (30 recommended) with renewals — giving it the operating
  and commercial rights to run a sustainable business **without ever taking title**
  to the grant-funded property.
- The structure is built to satisfy the TBCP Award, **2 CFR Part 200**, federal
  property / federal-interest / disposition rules, **Build America, Buy America**,
  NEPA/NHPA, and federal audit / record-retention / reporting requirements.

Service territory: **southeastern North Carolina** (exact area to be finalized).

### ⚠️ Threshold issue — read first
The Lumbee Tribe's **federal-recognition status** (Lumbee Act of 1956) directly affects
whether it qualifies as a **"Tribal Government"** eligible for TBCP. This package
**does not assume** eligibility is settled — it is flagged throughout as
`[GRANT COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED]` + `[ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED]` and is the
**lead item** on the Attorney Review Issue List, the Risk Matrix, and the Due
Diligence list. Confirm eligibility before incurring proposal costs.

- TBCP **Round 3** (opened June 17, 2026; applications due Sept 17, 2026; ~$540M;
  **no match required**; 100/20 Mbps service floor). Confirm against the live NOFO.
- The Lumbee Tribe is (or will be) an **eligible Tribal Government** — **unconfirmed; see threshold issue.**

- RIVR Tech is preliminarily treated as a **contractor/vendor** (not a subrecipient)
  under 2 CFR 200.331 — **subject to grant-counsel confirmation.**

  pricing, revenue-share %, reserved strand counts, fixed-payment amounts, transition

## 8. Instructions for updating placeholders

Every unresolved item is one of:

- **`[■ …]`** — a yellow-highlighted **placeholder** for missing data (a number, name, date, route).
- **`[BUSINESS DECISION REQUIRED]`** — a commercial choice for the principals.
- **`[ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED]`** — legal judgment / bracketed alternatives for counsel.
- **`[GRANT COMPLIANCE REVIEW REQUIRED]`** — confirm against the Award / 2 CFR 200.
- **`[TECHNICAL INFORMATION REQUIRED]`** — engineering/GIS data.

Two ways to resolve them:

1. **Directly in Word/Excel** — search each document for `[■`, `[BUSINESS`,
   `[ATTORNEY`, `[GRANT`, `[TECHNICAL`, replace with the agreed value, and remove
   the yellow highlight / flag. (Search the four bracket strings to find every one.)
2. **Regenerate from source (recommended for shared terms)** — canonical values live
   in `Scripts/common.py` (`DEAL`, party names, IRU terms, citations). Edit them once
   there, then re-run the relevant `Scripts/gen_*.py` generator; the change flows to
   every document consistently. After any edit, re-run
   `python3 Scripts/consistency_check.py` (should report **0 FAIL**).

## 9. Instructions for using the financial model

Open `04_Financial_Model/Lumbee_RIVR_Tech_IRU_Financial_Model.xlsx` and read the
**Instructions** tab first. Cell colors: **amber = input (edit these)**,
**blue = formula (don't edit)**, **green = output**.

Drive the model from three switches on the **Assumptions** tab:
- **Scenario** — `1` Low / `2` Base / `3` High
- **IRU term** — `20` / `25` / `30` (gates revenue & payments beyond the term)
- **Revenue-share option** — `1`–`5` = A–E (all five also shown side-by-side)

Then edit the amber input blocks (grant, homes passed, take-rate ramp, ARPU by
segment, churn, bad debt, opex, staffing, reserve %, discount rate, and the
option-specific payment inputs). Every result recalculates automatically — **you
never edit a formula.** The **Dashboard** tab shows headline outputs (Y30 revenue,
cumulative Tribal payments, RIVR Tech NPV/IRR, payback) and a green/red roll-up of
the 12 formula checks. Keep the model's option and term **in sync** with the
Financial & Revenue-Sharing Schedule (02.04).

> The workbook stores formulas; open it in Excel (or a spreadsheet that recalculates
> on load) to see computed values. Default placeholder economics are illustrative
> only and must be replaced with negotiated figures.
